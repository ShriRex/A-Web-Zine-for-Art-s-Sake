An Individual's constitution is such that they must not only find facts, but also some laws which will lighten the burden of mere number and quantity. There is yet another individual in me, not the physical, but the personal being; which has its likes and dislikes, and wants to find something to fulfil its needs of love. This personal being; is found in the region where we are free from all necessity, —above the needs, both of body and mind, —above the expedient and useful. This is the world from which Science turns away, and in which (Web)Zines takes its place. And if we can answer the question as to what a (web)zine is, we shall know what this world is with which a (web)zine has such intimate relationship.
